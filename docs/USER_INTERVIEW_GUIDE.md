# Beta User Interview Guide

Use after the task. Ask neutral follow-ups such as “What made you say that?” Do not reveal the intended product logic until all comprehension probes are complete.

## Warm-up

1. Tell me about the last time you compared two Chinese teas online.
2. What information was hard to understand or trust?

## Initial result

3. In your own words, what is Guancha recommending—or not recommending—right now?
4. What evidence seems most important to that judgment?
5. What is still uncertain?
6. Which parts do you believe came directly from the product page, and which are interpretation?

## Merchant questions

7. Which question, if any, would you actually send to a merchant? Why?
8. Which question feels unnecessary, repetitive, awkward, or costly to ask?
9. What answer would change your decision?

## Rejudge and correction

10. What changed after the simulated merchant reply?
11. Did anything change that should not have changed, or fail to change when it should have?
12. Does the change explanation increase or reduce trust? Why?

## Comparative value

13. If the product only returned OCR/extracted fields, what would be lost or gained?
14. Compared with sending both screenshots to a general AI, where is Guancha more useful, less useful, or the same?
15. At what point would you stop using this and decide on your own?

## Closing

16. What is the single most important change before you would use this for a real purchase?
17. Is there anything the system presented as more certain than it deserved?

Do not ask “Was the recommendation accurate?” without defining a verifiable ground truth. Capture concrete reasoning, not courtesy ratings alone.
