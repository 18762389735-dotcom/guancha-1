# Beta Hypotheses

These are five testable hypotheses, not confirmed findings.

## H1 — Explainable comparison

Chinese tea beginners can state, in their own words, why the first candidate is ranked ahead of another after reading Result. Evidence: observation + comprehension probe. Falsifier: most participants repeat a label but cannot connect Need, product evidence, and uncertainty.

## H2 — Evidence-source trust

Participants can distinguish product-page claims, bounded system interpretation, and merchant-provided facts. Evidence: source-identification task before prompting. Falsifier: participants treat inferred/merchant facts as independently verified product truth.

## H3 — Question value

At least one generated merchant question feels decision-relevant enough that participants would realistically ask it. Evidence: copy/intent observation plus non-leading interview reason. Falsifier: questions are seen as generic completeness checks or too costly to ask.

## H4 — Correctable decision value

After a simulated merchant reply, participants understand what changed or stayed unchanged in the unified rejudge and can use that to choose. Evidence: change-recall and final-choice explanation. Falsifier: Delta feels arbitrary or users cannot identify the new fact/risk.

## H5 — Value beyond OCR/general chat

Participants identify a concrete advantage over receiving extracted fields alone or sending screenshots to a general AI: comparable options, source boundaries, actionable questions, or auditable correction. Evidence: comparative interview after task completion. Falsifier: participants see no meaningful advantage beyond OCR or generic summarization.
